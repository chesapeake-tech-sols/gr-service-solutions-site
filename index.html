Hydraulic specialists

Leaks, drift and lost power, fixed right

Hydraulic problems rarely stay small. A weeping fitting becomes a burst hose; a tired seal becomes a cylinder that won't hold a load. We repair the failure and check what caused it.

  • System-level troubleshootingWe test pressure and flow to find the actual cause, not just the part that broke.
  • Cylinder reseals and rebuildsBooms, buckets, loaders, lifts and dump bodies.
  • Clean, safe workProper torque, routing and cleanup so the fix lasts.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you really come to us?

Yes. G&R is a mobile service. Our service truck carries the tools, diagnostic equipment and common parts to repair most machines right where they are: job site, farm field, yard or shop.

What kind of equipment do you work on?

Diesel and hydraulic equipment of all kinds: excavators, skid steers, loaders, tractors and ag machinery, dump and work trucks, forklifts, telehandlers and industrial hydraulic systems. If you're not sure, call and ask.

How do you price repairs?

We diagnose the problem first and give you a clear quote before any repair work begins. No surprises on the invoice.

Can you make hydraulic hoses on site?

We replace hoses and fittings in the field for most common sizes. For unusual assemblies we'll get the right part fast and come back to install it.

Do you offer maintenance plans for fleets?

Yes. We can set up regular preventive maintenance visits for contractors, farms and fleets so equipment gets serviced on a schedule that fits your work.

How far do you travel?

We cover the entire DMV: all of Maryland, Washington DC and Northern Virginia. Give us a call with your location and we'll get the truck scheduled.
